Aether Sovereign | VR-RAG
Aether Sovereign (VR-RAG) is a sovereign, voice-first, 3D spatial financial intelligence platform designed for Australian family offices and high-net-worth wealth managers. It combines:

Evidence-Grounded RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation): Ingests complex financial documents (PDFs, DOCX, XLSX spreadsheets, CSVs) and strictly grounds answers using vector search with zero hallucinations.
Australian Regulatory & Privacy Compliance: Formatted specifically for Australian financial contexts (AUD, ATO, Superannuation, Franking Credits) under Australian Privacy Principles (APPs, Privacy Act 1988) and ASIC RG 244 factual advice disclaimers.
Voice-First Interaction (Speech-to-Speech): Spoken voice input transcribed via Whisper STT, queried via RAG, and spoken back using Australian neural TTS (en-AU-NatashaNeural).
Spatial 3D & WebXR "View in VR": An Apple visionOS-inspired 3D room running in WebGL/Three.js with multi-device VR support (Desktop 3D, Meta Quest 3 6DoF, Apple Vision Pro, and Google Cardboard stereoscopic mobile view).
